what we are

What EBB is, and what it does for every payment that runs through it:

Between channel and rail

Every channel your customers touch on one side, every rail your payments reach on the other. EBB is the single resilient layer in between — tapping your core without ever becoming the record.

Your core stays the record

The system of truth stays exactly where it is — EBB never becomes the book of record.

Payments keep moving

When a rail goes down, EBB routes around it, retries and reverses — clearing without anyone being paged.

Matched and provable

Every transaction reconciled continuously, on an immutable, auditable trail on your own data.

what we believe

Four convictions we build on.

01

Banks should own their rails

Resilience and routing are too important to live inside a vendor’s black box. EBB runs in your estate, under your controls — yours to operate and yours to change.

02

Connectivity is commodity; resilience is the product

Anyone can reach a rail. Keeping payments moving when one fails — routing, retrying, reversing, reconciling — is the hard part, and the part we obsess over.

03

Engineered, not assembled

Built by engineers for the people who run payments at 2am. No glue-code sprawl, no integration-by-integration drift — one layer, designed to be operated.

04

No lock-in

A standard layer your bank runs, with your core as the record. Add a rail as a config change or a plugin — and leave on your own terms.
