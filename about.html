About EBB Systems

We build the payment layer banks own.

EBB Systems is the orchestration layer between every channel your customers touch and every rail they reach — engineered so the bank, not a vendor, stays in control. We are Engineered By Bytes: an engineering company building resilient payment infrastructure for regulated institutions worldwide.

what we are

One resilient layer, owned by the bank.

EBB sits between every channel — mobile app, USSD, agency, corporate host-to-host — and every operator your payments reach: mobile money, card networks, interbank switches, cross-border corridors. Your core stays the system of record; EBB taps it through a ledger interface and never becomes the book of record.

When a rail goes down, EBB routes around it, retries, and reverses automatically — payments keep clearing without anyone being paged. Every transaction is matched to the operator continuously, and the whole lifecycle is captured in an immutable, auditable trail on your own data.

The layer your team has been holding together by hand — engineered, owned by you, and built to stay up.

what we believe

Four convictions we build on.

01

Banks should own their rails

Resilience and routing are too important to live inside a vendor’s black box. EBB runs in your estate, under your controls — yours to operate and yours to change.

02

Connectivity is commodity; resilience is the product

Anyone can reach a rail. Keeping payments moving when one fails — routing, retrying, reversing, reconciling — is the hard part, and the part we obsess over.

03

Engineered, not assembled

Built by engineers for the people who run payments at 2am. No glue-code sprawl, no integration-by-integration drift — one layer, designed to be operated.

04

No lock-in

A standard layer your bank runs, with your core as the record. Add a rail as a config change or a plugin — and leave on your own terms, not ours.

who we build for

Regulated institutions, worldwide.

EBB is built for organisations that move other people’s money and answer to a regulator for it — on the core banking systems they already run.

Banks

Tier-1 to challenger banks running Finacle, T24, FlexCube, or a custom core — modernising payments without replacing the core.

Licensed fintechs & PSPs

Payment service providers and licensed fintechs that need bank-grade resilience, audit, and compliance on infrastructure they control.

Engineering teams

The teams who own the integration and carry the pager — who want one layer to operate, not a dozen point-to-point connections to maintain.
