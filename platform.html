The platform

Built once. Extended safely.

One resilient layer between your customers and every payment network — here it is in plain terms, then in full. You run it in your own estate; your core banking stays exactly as it is.

01
In plain termsMoney comes in, money goes out.

Think of EBB as a smart switchboard between your customers and every payment network — money flows both ways through it, and when a payment fails it fixes itself.

A customer paying you (money in, C2B), you paying a customer (money out, B2C), or business-to-business — EBB routes each one. Your core banking is your own separate system; EBB plugs into it and leaves it unchanged.

02
Resilient by defaultFour things the core does to every payment.

Applied automatically after a payment leaves your core — no human in the loop.

01 · Route

Route

Every payment sent down the right rail the moment it’s made — resilient routing, no manual steps.

02 · Retry

Retry

Timeouts and failures caught, backed off and re-attempted automatically — never paying twice.

03 · Reverse

Reverse

Payments that can’t clear are auto-reversed back to the core, fully audited — the customer is made whole.

04 · Reconcile

Reconcile

Every leg matched against the operator continuously — mismatches resolve before month-end.

03
The architectureChannels in, operators out — your core beside it.

The same story with the real components. The dashed frame is the EBB platform — what you run; your core banking sits outside it, separate.

→ scroll to see the full diagram

How to read it: every channel enters through the EBB gateway (one front door); the core orchestrates the payment and invokes the operator’s signed plugin to reach the network and settle. Your core banking is a separate system that EBB plugs into, untouched. Card authorization stays real-time on the card networks; EBB owns routing, retry, reversal, reconciliation, AML and audit.

04
Built once · extended safelyThree building blocks.
01

The core & gateway

Move money — resilient routing, retry, reverse. Keep the books true — reconcile, ledger sync. Prove & protect — signature verification, AML, audit.

02

The plugins

A small integration to one operator — signed at build, verified by the core before it loads. Mobile money, cards, inter-banking, cross-border.

03

How it deploys

Software your bank runs, inside your own estate, on a gateway and the cores you operate. Your core untouched; a new rail live in weeks; high availability.

05
Defence in depthSix layers, every transaction.

The controls a regulator can see — applied by the core itself.

Signatures

Every payload cryptographically signed — the message, not just the channel.

Mutual TLS

Both ends verified on every connection. No password fallback.

Network isolation

Each surface on separate, segmented ports — blast radius contained.

AML screening

Sanctions & AML checks at the infrastructure layer, before settle.

PCI-DSS masking

Sensitive fields masked at ingestion — never stored in the clear.

Audit trail

Every field, at every stage, logged — immutable and queryable.
