ii
The pluginssmall · signed · per operator

A plugin is a small backend-Java integration to one operator — written by your developers or ours. The author writes only the operator-specific code; the core orchestrates the rest.

Each plugin is signed when it’s built. On deploy, the core verifies that signature before the plugin ever loads — proving its integrity, authenticity and non-repudiation. A typical plugin connects to:

Mobile moneyWallet schemes such as M-Pesa, Airtel Money and MoMo.
Inter-bankingDomestic switches and rails like RTGS and ACH.
Card networksScheme rails such as Visa and Mastercard.
Cross-borderRemittance and FX settlement corridors.

how it deploys

A layer you own and operate.

EBB is software your bank runs — not a managed service you route through. You keep the deployment, the keys, and the system-of-record relationship.

In your estate

Runs on the infrastructure your bank controls — cloud, on-prem, or hybrid. Yours to operate.

Config, not rebuild

A new rail or corridor is a signed plugin and a configuration change — live in weeks, not a project per integration.

Your core, untouched

EBB integrates through a ledger interface to Finacle, T24, FlexCube, or a custom core — without replacing it.

defence in depth

Six structural layers, on every transaction.

Signatures, mutual TLS, network isolation, AML, PCI-DSS masking, and end-to-end audit — applied unconditionally by the core to every payment, not bolted on per integration.

01

Signatures

Every payload is cryptographically signed — the message itself, not just the channel.

02

Mutual TLS

Both parties are verified on every connection. No password fallback.

03

Network isolation

Surfaces run on separate, segmented ports — blast radius contained by design.

04

AML screening

Sanctions and AML checks at the infrastructure layer, before a payment settles.

05

PCI-DSS masking

Sensitive fields are masked at ingestion — never stored or logged in the clear.

06

Audit trail

Every field, at every stage, logged — immutable and queryable end to end.
