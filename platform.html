05
Defence in depthevery layer · every transaction

Six structural layers — applied by the core itself, to every transaction.

SignaturesSigned payloads

Every payload is cryptographically signed — the message itself, not just the channel.

Mutual TLSBoth ends verified

Both parties are verified on every connection. No password fallback.

Network isolationSegmented surfaces

Each surface runs on separate, segmented ports — blast radius contained by design.

AML screeningSanctions & AML

Sanctions and AML checks at the infrastructure layer, before a payment settles.

PCI-DSS maskingMasked at ingestion

Sensitive fields are masked on the way in — never stored or logged in the clear.

Audit trailImmutable log

Every field, at every stage, logged — immutable and queryable end to end.
